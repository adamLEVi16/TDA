How the strategy works — one monthly loop

Plain English: once a month, check each of 8 boring index ETFs. Keep the ones trending up (sized so no single one dominates the risk); dump the ones trending down into cash. That's it. No options, no leverage, no shorting — just deciding when to own ordinary assets.

When everything happens — the monthly cadence

The strategy touches the market one day a month. Signals use only month-end closes that already happened; the position then sits untouched until the next month-end. Rebalance-date tests show the exact day doesn't matter (trading 5, 10, even 21 sessions late still works).
