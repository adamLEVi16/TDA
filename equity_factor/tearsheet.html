What it does

Each month-end, for 8 liquid index ETFs: hold those above their 10-month moving average (sized by inverse volatility); move the rest to cash (T-bills). No options, leverage, shorting, or forecasting. Universe: SPY, EFA, EEM, TLT, IEF, GLD, DBC, VNQ. ~110%/yr turnover, ~10 bps cost.

Alpha verification (net of costs — alpha_test.py, extended_tests.py)

  • CAPM alpha +1.80%/yr, t = 2.30, p = 0.022 (β = 0.24), 451 months.
  • 1985–2024 extension (4-fund variant, incl. Volcker rate shock): +2.29%/yr, t = 3.08, p = 0.002.
  • Spanning test (vs all five underlying funds — could any static mix replicate it?): +1.40%/yr, p = 0.058 — borderline; stated, not rounded up.
  • Timing beats the identical static-weight portfolio (ΔSharpe +0.26, p = 0.025); stationary bootstrap p = 0.001–0.006 across block choices.
  • Survives rebalancing 5/10/21 sessions late (Sharpe 1.03–1.11) — no date luck.

Robustness (torture_test.py, extended_tests.py)

  • Trend window 6–14 mo → Sharpe 1.15–1.17 (no fitted parameters); survives 40 bps/side costs (1.06); drop any single asset → 0.96–1.32.
  • Recessions (NBER): strategy −2.4%/yr vs equity −13.4%/yr; rolling 5-year alpha positive in 84% of windows.
  • No look-ahead (last-month weights re-derived by hand); deterministic; weights+cash=1; no leverage/shorting.

Known limitations

  • The alpha is modest and episodic. ~1.8%/yr at β 0.24 overall; by decade it lived in 1997–2016 and was ≈ 0 in 2017–2024 (see chart). A diversifying sleeve (20–30%), not a return engine.
  • Gives up upside — severe career risk. 6.8%/yr vs equity 10.5%/yr; lags buy-and-hold in 69% of rolling 3-year windows, once for 159 consecutive months. This discomfort is likely why the premium persists.
  • Rising-rate regimes hurt (Sharpe 0.34 vs equity 0.52 when trailing bond returns are negative); the bond sleeve had a falling-rate tailwind for much of the sample.
  • Long-only, not a true hedge; monthly rebalancing cannot dodge fast crashes (Oct-1987: −11.8% in one month) and re-enters late after V-recoveries (2020).
  • Edge vs a good 60/40 is only borderline (p ≈ 0.07); decisive only vs pure equity buy-and-hold.
